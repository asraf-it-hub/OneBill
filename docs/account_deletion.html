OneBill Account & Data Deletion

Official Account Deletion Policy & Request Portal

Option 1: Delete Directly in OneBill App (Recommended)

The fastest way to permanently delete your account and immediately purge all data from both your device and cloud servers is through the app:

  1. Open OneBill on your Android phone and sign in.
  2. Tap the top-right menu icon (⋮) and select Settings.
  3. Scroll down past Data & Sync to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account, review the warnings, type DELETE to confirm, and submit.
Data Deletion Scope & Retention Disclosures
Data that is permanently expunged:
  • Your login credentials, auth profile, and identity records.
  • All businesses and workspaces owned by your account.
  • All customer profiles, contacts, and phone details.
  • All invoice details, item lines, payment records, and receipts.
  • All income and expense bookkeeping records.
  • Cloud backups, synchronization receipts, and local SQLite caches.

Statutory Retention Notice: In accordance with Indian tax laws (such as GST regulations), business transaction invoices may be subject to legal retention obligations where applicable. Any non-statutory data, identity tokens, customer books, and device records are completely destroyed immediately upon deletion.

Option 2: Submit an External Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit a verifiable deletion request below. An identity confirmation link will be delivered to your registered email address to verify account ownership.
